# Service scene accuracy

These decorative scenes show illustrative equipment, not live readings or installation instructions.

- **Outlet:** `RATED 15 A · 125 V` is a receptacle equipment rating, not a claimed measured supply voltage. Leviton lists NEMA 5-15R duplex receptacles with this rating: https://leviton.com/products/5325-SE
- **Tester:** the non-contact pen lights to indicate voltage presence; it does not display a measured voltage or establish correct wiring/absence of voltage. https://www.fluke.com/en-us/learn/blog/electrical/non-contact-voltage-detector-basics
- **Panel:** `EXAMPLE · 120/240 V AC` describes a typical residential split-phase service, not every building. PG&E Greenbook: https://www.pge.com/content/dam/pge/docs/account/service-requests/greenbook-manual-full.pdf
- **EV:** the scene shows typical home Level 2 service at nominal 240 V AC. Commercial Level 2 can use 208 V. Removed the fixed 7.7 kW claim because actual charging power depends on current, supply and vehicle. https://afdc.energy.gov/fuels/electricity-stations
- **Energy pulses:** stylized energy delivery toward the car, not literal electron motion or DC in the Level 2 AC cable. The vehicle's onboard charger converts AC to DC for the battery. https://www.energy.gov/cmei/femp/articles/electric-vehicle-supply-equipment-infrastructure-federal-fleet-training
- **Meter:** 120 V AC is explicitly an example; actual voltage varies. The selector stays on AC voltage while connected. Removed the green checkmark because that reading does not establish wiring safety.
